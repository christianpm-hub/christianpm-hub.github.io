Biography

Emmanuel Gazmey Santiago, known professionally as Anuel AA, was born on November 26, 1992, in Carolina, Puerto Rico. Raised in a family deeply connected to the music industry—his father, José Gazmey, was vice president of Sony Music Puerto Rico—Anuel was exposed to music production and business from an early age.

Inspired by American hip-hop and the emerging Latin urban sound, he began writing and recording music in his late teens, determined to create a raw, unfiltered style that reflected the realities of street life.

Anuel AA emerged as one of the pioneer figures of the Latin Trap movement. Blending aggressive hip-hop beats with explicit lyrics about street credibility, heartbreak, and loyalty, he quickly gained a massive underground following with tracks like "Esclava" and "Sola".

His signature ad-lib, "Real Hasta la Muerte" (Real Until Death), became more than just a catchphrase—it turned into a cultural movement and a core philosophy for his fan base.

Despite facing major legal hurdles early in his career, Anuel's fan base grew exponentially. In 2018, on the day of his release from prison, he dropped his debut studio album, Real Hasta la Muerte. The album became an immediate commercial success, topping the Billboard Top Latin Albums chart and solidifying his position at the forefront of the Latin urban genre.

Following his debut, Anuel released a series of massive global hits and high-profile collaborations with artists such as Daddy Yankee, Bad Bunny, Karol G, Ozuna, and J Balvin. His subsequent albums—Emmanuel (2020), Las Leyendas Nunca Mueren (2021), and LLNM2—further demonstrated his ability to dominate global streaming platforms and headline arenas worldwide.

Anuel AA remains one of the most influential and controversial figures in modern Latin music. As a key architect of Latin Trap, he helped bridge the gap between American hip-hop culture and the Latin urban scene, paving the way for a new generation of Latin artists.
